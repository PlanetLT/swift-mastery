The watch face is the real home screen. A complication is the small slot your app can occupy there. People may never open your full app if the complication answers the question. That is a success, not a failure.

ClockKit was the old API. New complications are WidgetKit accessory widgets. If you find a ClockKit sample, use Apple's migration article rather than copying the sample. The idea is unchanged: a timeline of entries, a family of shapes, a placeholder.

## One string in the slot

A widget extension returns a timeline. For a study app, the entry is the next lesson's short title, and the refresh is "when the lesson changes" plus a backup date. The placeholder is the word "Study" or a dash, not a fake lesson title that looks real.

Accessory families are the shapes: circular, corner, rectangular, inline. Design the rectangular one and the circular one. Do not assume a long title will fit the circular face. `watchTitle` from the glance lesson is the field you show.

Tap on the complication opens the app, often to a specific lesson if you add a widget URL. Deep-link to the next lesson, not to a settings page.

<Callout title="Where people stall">

They show four fields in a corner slot. Or they update the complication by launching the app every minute. WidgetKit wants a timeline, and the system budgets reloads. Provide the next few entries if you can predict them, such as a timer's end. A lesson title that changes only when the person taps Done can wait for a reload you request from the app after the save.

</Callout>

## Exercise

Write the complication's text for three states: no lessons, one lesson titled "Optionals", and a title longer than twenty characters. Design what the circular and rectangular families show in each state. Only then create the widget target.

<Solution>

No lessons: rectangular "Nothing queued", circular a simple symbol. One lesson: rectangular "Optionals", circular the first letter or a book symbol plus a short word if it fits. Long title: a shortened `watchTitle` you stored on purpose, not a scaled paragraph. Create the WidgetKit extension after those strings exist. Use the accessory families in the widget configuration. Include a placeholder entry that does not pretend a lesson is scheduled.

</Solution>
